What is volleyball?

Volleyball is a team sport played over a net. Two teams try to land the ball on the other side without letting it touch the floor. Every player helps, and everyone gets to touch the ball!

6 players a side

Each team has six players on the court. Teamwork is what wins matches.

Three touches

A team can touch the ball up to three times before sending it over the net.

No ball left behind

Rally, dig, set, spike. Every skill has its own name and its own fun.
